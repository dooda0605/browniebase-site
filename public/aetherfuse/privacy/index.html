Effective date: October 21, 2026

Browniebase ("we") operates the game Aetherfuse (Korean title 에테르퓨즈, the "Game") and complies with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws. You can play without signing in; in that case your game progress stays on your device and is not sent to us. If the English and Korean versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

  • Data on your device: until you delete the Game or its data.

4. Ads and your choices

The Game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalization, in your device settings. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), personalized ads are shown only with your consent, and we do not collect usage events.

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or to stop its processing. To delete your account and all server data, follow browniebase.com/aetherfuse/delete-account or email us. We respond without undue delay.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea). We do not knowingly collect personal information from them; if we learn we have, we delete it.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our database is accessible only through our server functions, not directly from the app.

8. Changes

We will announce changes on this page and in the Game at least 7 days before they take effect.
