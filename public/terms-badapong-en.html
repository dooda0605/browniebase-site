1. Service Overview

Badapong is a service providing underwater visibility grades (A~E), ocean weather forecasts, and external tour booking links for divers, snorkelers, freedivers, and beach swimmers. It also shows users the lessons, tours and other offerings posted by partners (dive shops, surf shops and other businesses) and forwards users' applications to them. In transactions between partners and users, the Operator is a mail-order brokerage intermediary under the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, etc. (the "E-Commerce Act") and is not a party to the mail-order sale.

We are not liable for accidents resulting from reliance on Badapong information.
